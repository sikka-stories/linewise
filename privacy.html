Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Linewise is a personal project by Shreyas Shinde. Contact: [support email to be added before release].

The short version

There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers and no selling of data. In the apps, your progress and notebooks stay on your device. AI features are off until you choose to turn them on.

What stays on your device

Notebooks, imported PDFs, settings and, in the iOS and Android apps, your progress. We cannot see these unless you choose to send something.

What the AI features send

If you turn AI on, the questions you ask, your typed work, images of the lines you write by hand, pages of any question PDF you attach, and details of the topic you are on are sent to our server and passed to Google's Gemini API to get a reply. Our server does not keep this content, except for replies you choose to report.

We use Google's free tier. Under Google's terms, Google may use what is sent to improve its products and machine learning, and people at Google may read it. Please do not include your name, your school or anything else personal. See the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy.

If you add your own Gemini key, AI requests go directly from your device to Google under your own agreement with Google, and do not pass through our server.

What our server stores

  • Website progress. If you use the website, your practice attempts, flagged grades and study time are stored against a random ID made on your device. We store only a one-way hash of that ID. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Reports. If you report an AI reply, we store the reply, the question before it, your reason, any note you add and the same hashed ID, so that it can be reviewed.
  • Daily limit counters. To share the free AI allowance fairly we count requests per day against salted hashes of the device ID and IP address. IP addresses are not stored in the database, and counters are removed after the day ends.

Our hosting providers, Render (server) and Neon (database), process this data on our behalf. They and we may hold ordinary short-lived server logs. Our logs do not contain your questions or answers.

Videos

Lessons can show YouTube videos. Their preview pictures load from YouTube's image server when a lesson opens, which lets YouTube see your device's internet address. The video itself loads only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, or in the apps it opens in YouTube. YouTube's own policies apply.

Who it is for

On this website the AI features use your own Gemini key, so Google's terms for that key apply to you, including its age requirements. Linewise is made for adult learners and is not directed at children. Linewise is not currently offered in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom.

Keeping and deleting your data

Website progress is kept until you delete it. Reports are kept until they have been reviewed, for at most 12 months. To delete everything at any time, open Settings, then "AI and your data", then "delete all my data". This clears the device and the server at once and cannot be undone. Because there are no accounts, we cannot tell which stored data is yours from an email alone, so please use the in-app option while you still have the device. You can still contact us with any question about your data. Uninstalling the app removes what is on the device, but not anything stored on the server, so use the delete option first. What Google has already received cannot be withdrawn by us.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. The AI key is held on the server and is never in the app. Each device's data can only be reached with that device's own random ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change, and important changes will be shown in the app.
